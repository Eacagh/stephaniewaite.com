Welcome

Welcome to the website of violinist Stephanie Waite. Here you can find information about her performing work, her teaching, and her chamber music collaborations.

Stephanie is a busy professional violinist, performing all over the UK and abroad, and teaching from her home in Bedford. She works regularly with leading British orchestras and chamber ensembles, and is a passionate chamber musician and coach.

To read Stephanie’s full biography, please visit the About page. For professional and teaching details in list form, see the CV page. To get in touch directly, please use the Contact page.
